Support

How can we help?

How do I upload my NRIC?

Place the card on a flat surface, use good lighting and make sure all four corners are visible.

How do I complete the selfie check?

Look directly at the camera, keep your face inside the frame and remove any mask or dark glasses.

Why is my information collected?

It is used for identity verification, fraud prevention and regulatory screening.

What happens during manual review?

A compliance officer checks uncertain information before making the final decision.
